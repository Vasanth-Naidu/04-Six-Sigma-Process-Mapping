# Portfolio Module 04: Quality Architecture, Process Blueprinting & Practical Six Sigma Execution (iGate Global Solutions)

## Executive Summary:
* **Domain:** Quality Assurance Architecture, Pre-Sales Process Blueprinting, Practical Six Sigma (DMAIC), Gemba Walks & Operational Quality Adherence
* **Company Context:** iGate Global Solutions (formerly Quintant Services Limited — BPO & Analytics Division)
* **Role:** Quality Lead & Process Excellence Architect
* **Core Value Delivered:** Dual-focused on pre-sales quality engineering and active BPO process governance. Partnered with Business Development (BD) teams on RFP engagements to design end-to-end client process flows, establish error-opportunity matrices, and build production QA audit sheets. Managed quality adherence across live BPO processes using Gemba Walks, 2-tier QA tracking, root-cause refresher training, and practical Six Sigma (DMAIC) training for frontline staff.

---

## 1. Operational Challenge & Scope:

* **Pre-Sales Process Blueprinting (RFP Stage):** Prospective BPO clients required detailed, risk-mitigated operational blueprints, clear error-opportunity maps, and robust QA audit frameworks prior to contract execution.
* **Quality Adherence & Defect Leakage:** Newly transitioned BPO processes faced quality variances, requiring structured audit tracking to prevent error propagation into production queues.
* **Subjective QA Auditing & Refresher Gaps:** Disconnected audit tracking obscured core error patterns, hindering targeted refresher training for frontline processors.
* **Process Waste & Unobserved Operational Friction:** Standard documentation failed to capture actual floor execution pain points, manual workarounds, and non-value-added steps.

---

## 2. In-House Quality Engineering & Process Blueprinting Framework:

1. **Pre-Sales RFP Solutioning & Quality Design** — Collaborated with BD/Sales teams during client RFP discussions to translate requirements into end-to-end process maps, identify potential production error failure points, and design customized QA audit scorecards for client pitch proposals.
2. **Gemba Walk Process Observation & Waste Identification** — Conducted silent, non-intrusive Gemba Walks behind frontline processors to observe real-world execution, note personal pain points, map unrecorded operational issues, and pinpoint automation opportunities.
3. **Define & Measure (SIPOC, VOC & Data Collection)** — Established Project Charters, mapped high-level SIPOC boundaries, captured Pre & Post Voice of the Customer (VOC) feedback, and executed structured Data Collection Plans paired with Time-and-Motion studies.
4. **Analyze (Practical Root-Cause Analysis)** — Applied practical, intuitive statistical tools including Pareto Charts (80/20 rule), Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagrams, and 5 Whys Analysis to isolate vital error drivers without over-complicating frontline interpretation.
5. **Improve (Error-Proofing & Brainstorming)** — Facilitated team brainstorming sessions to design Error-Proofing (Poka-Yoke) controls, streamline To-Be process flows, eliminate non-value-added steps, and build automated post-solution data collection mechanisms.
6. **Control (Statistical Quality Control & SOP Governance)** — Built Control Charts, maintained rigorous Control Plans, updated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and delivered practical Six Sigma and MS Excel training to frontline teams to lock in quality gains.

---

## 3. Core Process Excellence Toolkit Built:

* **Pre-Sales QA Audit Frameworks:** Customized QA audit sheets and error-opportunity identification matrices developed for BD client RFPs.
* **Gemba Walk Flowcharts & VSMs:** Observational process maps capturing real-world execution friction, manual workarounds, and waste.
* **Root-Cause Analysis Engine (Pareto & Fishbone):** Practical defect classification models isolating top error drivers for targeted refresher training.
* **Poka-Yoke & Control Governance Suite:** Error-proofing controls, Control Charts, and updated SOPs enforcing long-term operational quality adherence.
